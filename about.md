Plainfix is a practical tech publication built for people who want a direct answer to a specific digital problem without filler, pop-ups, or vague advice.

Whether an online application form rejects your photo because of its kilobyte size, an iPhone `.heic` image refuses to open on Windows, a suspicious QR code or WhatsApp `.apk` file arrives on your phone, or your Google account hits its 15 GB storage ceiling, each guide on Plainfix walks through the exact steps and tells you what you will see on screen after every action.

## What We Cover

Plainfix organizes its guides into six focused areas:

1. **[Online Safety & Scams]({{ '/category/online-safety/' | relative_url }}):** Spotting QR code phishing (quishing), Indian UPI and "digital arrest" scam calls, malicious WhatsApp APK files, fake shopping stores, and email or password breaches.
2. **[Privacy, Accounts & AI]({{ '/category/privacy-ai/' | relative_url }}):** Setting up passkeys, checking whether an image is AI-generated, removing GPS and EXIF metadata from photos, testing whether online tools upload your files, and using AI chatbots safely at work.
3. **[Photos & Images]({{ '/category/images/' | relative_url }}):** Converting HEIC photos to JPG, resizing photos and signatures to exact KB and pixel requirements, preparing passport-size photos at home, comparing image formats, and removing backgrounds cleanly.
4. **[PDF & Documents]({{ '/category/pdf/' | relative_url }}):** Compressing PDFs under 1 MB, 500 KB, or 200 KB, scanning multi-page documents with a phone, removing passwords from PDFs you own, signing PDFs without printing, and copying text from scans with OCR.
5. **[Phone, Google & Internet Fixes]({{ '/category/everyday-tech/' | relative_url }}):** Freeing up full Google 15 GB and phone storage in a safe order, understanding Mbps vs MB/s download speeds, sharing Wi-Fi without reading out the password, and printing reliable shop QR codes.
6. **[Text, Data & Developer Basics]({{ '/category/text-data/' | relative_url }}):** Cleaning messy copied text, checking character limits for social posts and SMS, fixing invalid JSON and AI output errors, converting CSV and Excel files without losing leading zeros, and converting Unix timestamps.

## Ownership and Tool Disclosure

Most guides on Plainfix do not mention or link to any owned product.

The team that runs Plainfix also builds and operates three free web-based utility sites:

- **[PixelKaro](https://pixelkaro.com/):** Browser-based image and PDF utilities (such as photo resizing, HEIC conversion, metadata removal, and PDF compression).
- **[QRizeo](https://qrizeo.com/):** QR code utilities (such as QR inspection, Wi-Fi QR generation, UPI QR creation, and print size calculation).
- **[SnipFerry](https://snipferry.com/):** Text, JSON, CSV/Excel, and timestamp formatting utilities.

Plainfix is **not** independent of PixelKaro, QRizeo, or SnipFerry—they are run by the same owner. Whenever a guide on Plainfix links to one of those three sites:

1. We include a visible ownership disclosure immediately after the paragraph containing the first link.
2. We also explain at least one standard, non-owned method (such as built-in phone settings, operating-system tools, or standard desktop software) so you can complete the task without using our tools.
