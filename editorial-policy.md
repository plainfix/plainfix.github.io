Plainfix publishes step-by-step guides designed to solve specific, real-world technical problems with online safety, privacy, photos, PDFs, phones, and data formats. This page explains our editorial standards, how AI assistance is used and reviewed, how we cite sources, and our relationship with companion tool websites.

## 1. How We Choose Topics

Every article on Plainfix addresses a single, concrete problem where readers frequently run into errors or confusing advice—such as resizing an application signature to a strict kilobyte range, checking a QR code before scanning, freeing up a full 15 GB Google account, or fixing invalid JSON output. We avoid generic overview posts when a direct troubleshooting workflow is what the reader needs.

## 2. Human Review, Fact-Checking, and AI Assistance

Plainfix uses AI writing and drafting assistance to help structure outlines, tables, and initial drafts. **No article is published automatically or without human review.**

Before any guide is set to live (`published: true`), **Sebi Bano** manually reviews and fact-checks the article:

- **Step verification:** Menu names, settings paths, keyboard shortcuts, and formulas are checked against official vendor documentation and real device behavior.
- **Time-sensitive facts:** Platform storage limits, character limits, operating-system menu paths, and official photo specifications carry a `Last checked: [Month YYYY]` note so readers know when the detail was verified.
- **Real screenshots and examples:** We add original SVG diagrams and real verification screenshots rather than decorative stock imagery.
- **No invented data:** We never invent benchmark numbers, survey statistics, quotes, or product test scores.

## 3. Primary Sources and Citations

Every guide ends with a **Sources** section linking to two to five primary references. We prioritize:

- Official platform and operating-system documentation (Google Help, Apple Support, Microsoft Learn, Android Help).
- Technical standards and specifications (IETF RFCs, W3C, MDN Web Docs, Unicode Consortium, FIDO Alliance, C2PA / Content Credentials).
- Official government and public-interest advisories (such as CERT-In, NPCI, UIDAI, and the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal `cybercrime.gov.in`).

## 4. Safety, Financial, and Legal-Adjacent Topics

Guides covering online scams, UPI fraud, fake shopping sites, password breaches, or password-protected documents provide calm, practical technical steps and point strictly to official helplines and government portals. They are general informational guides, not legal or financial advice.

## 5. Ownership and Promotional Link Policy

The owner of Plainfix also builds and operates three free web utility sites: **PixelKaro** (`pixelkaro.com`), **QRizeo** (`qrizeo.com`), and **SnipFerry** (`snipferry.com`). Plainfix is **not** independent of these three sites.

To keep our guides trustworthy and genuinely useful:

1. **Most guides contain no promotional links:** The majority of articles on Plainfix do not link to PixelKaro, QRizeo, or SnipFerry at all.
2. **Strict relevance and placement:** Where a companion tool directly solves a step in a guide, we link only to the specific tool page where it helps—never in the opening paragraph and never in headings.
3. **Immediate disclosure:** Right after the first paragraph that links to PixelKaro, QRizeo, or SnipFerry, we display a clear disclosure box stating that the tool is built by the same team that runs Plainfix.
4. **Non-owned alternatives always included:** Every guide that mentions one of our tools also explains at least one non-owned way to accomplish the task using built-in phone features, operating-system utilities, or standard software.
5. **No paid links or undisclosed sponsorships:** Plainfix does not sell backlinks, accept paid guest posts, or participate in link exchanges.

## 6. Corrections and Updates

Technology interfaces change frequently. When an operating system update, browser release, or official portal rule changes a workflow in our guides, we update the affected steps and display an **Updated** date alongside the original publication date in the article header.

If you spot an error or outdated instruction, please email [plainfix.contact@gmail.com](mailto:plainfix.contact@gmail.com) with the guide title and details.
