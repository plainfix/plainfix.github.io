No police force, court, customs department, or investigative agency in India has the legal authority to place a citizen under "digital arrest" over a phone or video call. If someone calls claiming your Aadhaar or mobile number is linked to an illegal parcel or money-laundering case and demands you stay on a video call or transfer funds for "verification," disconnect immediately—it is an organized impersonation scam.

*Note: This guide provides general security and technical awareness information, not legal or financial advice. For any active cybercrime or banking fraud, contact your bank and official government helplines immediately.*

## How the "Digital Arrest" Scam Script Works Step by Step

Impersonation scammers rely on sudden authority and isolation so you do not pause to verify their story. Nearly every reported call follows the same four-stage script:

1. **The initial hook (IVR or courier call):** You receive an automated call claiming to be from a courier company (such as FedEx or DHL), TRAI, or Customs. The caller states that all your mobile numbers will be disconnected within two hours or that a parcel addressed to you containing passports, drugs, or SIM cards was intercepted.
2. **The transfer to a fake officer:** When you say you never sent such a parcel, the caller offers to "help" by transferring your call directly to a supposed Cyber Crime, Mumbai Police, CBI, Narcotics, or Enforcement Directorate officer.
3. **The fake video call and isolation:** The scammer switches to a WhatsApp or Skype video call wearing a uniform in front of a backdrop resembling a police station, sends forged arrest warrants bearing official-looking seals on WhatsApp, and orders you to stay on camera continuously ("digital arrest") without speaking to your spouse, family, or bank staff.
4. **The "verification transfer" demand:** Finally, the caller claims the only way to clear your name is to transfer your savings—or even take an instant personal loan—into a "Reserve Bank / Supreme Court supervision account" that they promise will be refunded after audit.

## UPI Fraud Traps: Collect Requests, PINs, and Screen-Sharing Apps

Alongside fake law-enforcement calls, scammers use three everyday UPI tricks to drain bank accounts in seconds:

- **"Enter your UPI PIN to receive money":** Whether someone claims to be sending you an electricity refund, a cashback reward, a lottery prize, or an advance payment for furniture you listed online, **you never need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money**. Entering your 4-digit or 6-digit UPI PIN authorizes an outgoing debit from your account.
- **Disguised UPI Collect Requests:** Scammers send a UPI "Collect" (Request Money) notification where the note field reads *"Credited Rs 5,000 — click Pay to accept."* Tapping **Pay** and typing your PIN sends Rs 5,000 to the scammer. If someone asks you to scan a payment code, read our guide on how to [check a QR code before you scan or pay]({{ '/guides/check-qr-code-before-scanning/' | relative_url }}).
- **Screen-sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk, QuickSupport):** Callers pretending to be bank KYC staff or utility-bill helpers ask you to install a remote-desktop app so they can watch your screen, read incoming bank OTP SMS messages in real time, and initiate transfers from your banking app.

## What to Do in the First Hour (Step-by-Step Action Plan)

If you or a family member received a scam call—or if money has already left your account—follow these steps in exact order:

1. **Disconnect the call and turn off any screen-sharing app immediately.** Do not ask the caller's permission to hang up, and do not delete the call log or WhatsApp messages—you will need those screenshots as evidence. If you installed a remote-access app, turn on **Airplane Mode** for 30 seconds while you uninstall that app from **Settings → Apps**.
2. **Dial 1930 immediately if any money was transferred.** Calling **1930** connects you to the official **National Cyber Crime Helpline** operated under the Ministry of Home Affairs (I4C) (Last checked: November 2026 [VERIFY]). Keep your 12-digit **UTR / UPI Transaction ID**, the exact transaction timestamp, your bank name, and the recipient's UPI ID or bank account number ready. You will receive an acknowledgment SMS once the operator logs your alert into the national financial fraud freeze system.
3. **File a formal report at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/).** Open the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, click **Report Cyber Crime**, sign in with your mobile number, and upload screenshots of the transaction receipt, the caller's phone number, and any forged documents sent on WhatsApp. You will see a formal Complaint Acknowledgment Number generated on screen.
4. **Call your bank's official 24×7 fraud helpline.** Use only the customer-care number printed on the back of your physical debit/credit card or inside your official banking app (never search Google for customer-care numbers, as scammers buy search ads for fake helpline numbers). Ask the bank to freeze your UPI ID, net banking, or compromised card and log a dispute against the fraudulent transaction reference.
5. **Report suspicious numbers even if you did not lose money via Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu).** If you recognized the scam and hung up without paying, report the number and WhatsApp screenshot on the Department of Telecommunications' **[Sanchar Saathi — Chakshu portal](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/)** (Last checked: November 2026 [VERIFY]) so telecom operators can investigate and block the scammer's SIM and device.

## Comparison: Real Law Enforcement & Banks vs Scammers

| Situation | What Real Police / Agencies / Banks Do | What Scammers Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Notices & Summons** | Serve official physical notices or through verified legal procedure; you can verify in person at a real local police station | Send photos of "warrants" on WhatsApp and forbid you from hanging up or visiting your local police station |
| **Video Calls** | Never conduct arrests, bail hearings, or showroom-style interrogations on WhatsApp or Skype | Keep you on a continuous video call ("digital arrest") and demand you show your room or bank balance |
| **Financial "Verification"** | Never ask citizens to transfer savings to an RBI or government "supervision account" | Demand RTGS, NEFT, or UPI transfers promising the money will be returned in 30 minutes |
| **Receiving Refunds / Cashbacks** | Credit funds directly to your account without needing your UPI PIN | Send a UPI Collect Request or QR code and tell you to enter your UPI PIN |
| **KYC or Bill Updates** | Handled inside your official bank app or verified branch | Ask you to install AnyDesk/TeamViewer or a `.apk` file sent on WhatsApp |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Staying on the call "just to prove you are innocent":** Scammers use scripted psychological pressure over several hours to exhaust you. Hang up in the first ten seconds and speak with a family member.
- **Calling back the number that just called you:** Caller IDs can be spoofed or routed through VoIP gateways. Always verify claims independently through official government websites.
- **Paying "recovery agents" on social media:** No private hacker or Instagram/Telegram recovery service can reverse a UPI transaction; anyone asking for a fee to recover lost scam money is running a second scam.

## If It Still Doesn't Work

- **The 1930 helpline line is busy in your state:** Do not wait on hold repeatedly—open **[cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/)** in your browser immediately and submit your financial fraud report online, as it feeds into the exact same Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System (CFCFRMS).
- **You already shared your Aadhaar or PAN photo on WhatsApp:** Log in to the official UIDAI **[myAadhaar portal](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/)** (Last checked: November 2026 [VERIFY]) and enable **Biometric Lock** so nobody can use your Aadhaar number for biometric SIM issuance or AePS withdrawals.

## Privacy and Safety Notes

To reduce targeted scam calls, avoid posting your phone number publicly on social media comments or public resumes, and enable WhatsApp's built-in privacy feature under **Settings → Privacy → Calls → Silence Unknown Callers** so WhatsApp voice/video calls from unsaved international or unknown numbers do not ring loudly. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Indian police or CBI ever arrest someone on a video call?
No. Under Indian law, there is no concept of "digital arrest" or online video detention. Any caller claiming to keep you under surveillance via WhatsApp, Skype, or Zoom is a criminal impersonator.

### What happens if I accidentally click a UPI Collect Request but do not enter my UPI PIN?
No money can leave your bank account via a standard UPI Collect Request unless you enter your secret 4-digit or 6-digit UPI PIN. Simply tap **Decline** and block the sender ID inside your UPI app.

### Why is reporting within the first hour ("Golden Hour") so important?
When you report a fraudulent transfer on **1930** or **cybercrime.gov.in** immediately, an automated electronic lien alert is sent to the receiving bank and any downstream banks or wallets before the fraudsters can withdraw the cash at an ATM or convert it.

### Where should I report a scam call if I hung up and lost zero money?
Use the **Chakshu (Report Suspected Fraud Communication)** facility on the official government **[Sanchar Saathi portal](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/)** within 30 days of receiving the call or WhatsApp message.
