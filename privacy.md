*Last updated: November 16, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **Plainfix** (`https://plainfix.github.io`) handles information when you visit our website.

## 1. No Accounts, Tracking Cookies, or Third-Party Ad Scripts

Plainfix is a static website built with Jekyll:

- **No user accounts or forms:** We do not ask you to register, log in, or submit personal data through web forms.
- **No tracking cookies or ad networks:** Plainfix does not set advertising, behavioral profiling, or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not load third-party analytics or advertising scripts by default. If privacy-focused analytics are ever added in the future, this policy will be updated before activation.
- **In-browser interactive widgets:** Interactive calculators and checklists on Plainfix (such as the download-time calculator or scam red-flag checker) run entirely inside your browser using local JavaScript. The values you enter are not transmitted to us.

## 2. Hosting by GitHub Pages

Plainfix is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When your browser requests a page or image from Plainfix, GitHub's servers receive standard technical request data—including your IP address, browser user-agent string, referring page, and timestamp—for security, reliability, and network operation purposes.

Please refer to the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) [VERIFY] for details on how GitHub processes visitor connection logs on GitHub Pages sites.

## 3. External Links and Companion Tool Sites

Our guides contain citations to official documentation and, in select articles with explicit disclosure, links to companion utility websites run by the same owner (**PixelKaro**, **QRizeo**, and **SnipFerry**). When you click a link to an external website, that site's own privacy policy governs how it handles your visit.

## 4. Email Correspondence

If you choose to contact us by email at [plainfix.contact@gmail.com](mailto:plainfix.contact@gmail.com), we will see your email address and any information you include in your message. We use that correspondence solely to reply to your inquiry or improve our guides, and we never sell or share your email address with marketers.

## 5. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, email [plainfix.contact@gmail.com](mailto:plainfix.contact@gmail.com).
