We welcome corrections, reports of changed menu names on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, or web apps, and questions about the steps in any published guide.

## Email

You can reach **Sebi Bano** and the Plainfix editorial desk by email:

- **Email:** [plainfix.contact@gmail.com](mailto:plainfix.contact@gmail.com)

Plainfix does not use contact forms or support tickets so that we do not collect or store visitor form submissions on third-party servers.

## What to Include When Reporting an Issue

If a step in a guide did not match what you saw on your screen, including the following details helps us verify and update the article quickly:

1. **The URL or title of the guide** you were reading.
2. **Your device and operating system version** (for example, Android 16, iOS 19, Windows 11, or macOS) or browser name.
3. **Which step number changed** and the exact menu label you saw instead.

Please do **not** send passwords, one-time passcodes (OTPs), bank account statements, Aadhaar numbers, or unredacted personal documents by email. If you are experiencing an active online financial scam or unauthorized bank transaction, contact your bank immediately and use official government reporting channels (such as dialing **1930** or visiting **cybercrime.gov.in** in India).
