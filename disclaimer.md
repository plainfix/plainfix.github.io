- **Document passwords and signatures:** Guides explaining how to remove a password from a PDF (such as a bank statement or e-Aadhaar) or how to sign a PDF apply strictly to documents that you lawfully own or are explicitly authorized to open and sign.

## 2. Official Specifications Always Take Precedence

When preparing photos, signatures, or PDF documents for government portals, passport applications, or competitive examinations, always read and follow the exact pixel, kilobyte, format, and background rules printed in your current official notification. Plainfix explains the technical method to reach a target size, but individual portals can change their rules at any time.
